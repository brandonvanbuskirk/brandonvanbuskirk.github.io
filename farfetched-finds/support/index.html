Log offline. Earn points right away.

Your points do not wait for a connection. iPhones and iPads, including Wi-Fi-only iPads, can save finds and score them on the device. Internet is needed to share those changes with another device and upload an iCloud backup.

Quick Log can reuse the city or region saved on this device, or you can choose a location from the offline catalog. The saved place stays available after restarting the app and during a trip. Check it as you travel: a saved city does not follow the car.

  1. Before leaving: update every participating device and join the same Family Party from Party if you want shared standings.
  2. While logging: check the visible location and use Change as you travel. If no location is saved, select one before logging. City/region scoring uses an approximate center.
  3. Points add immediately: the credited scout’s totals and the party standings on this device include the find right away. Other devices catch up after synchronization.
  4. When internet returns: keep the app open briefly or reopen it. A find waiting for Family Party delivery has already earned its points. Its delivery message clears after a successful upload; reconnecting does not rescore it from your new location or award it twice.

You can play locally without joining a Family Party. Automatic GPS can supply a location when available; City / Region is the manual option when GPS is unavailable or you prefer to choose a place. Keep the location shown in Quick Log accurate before saving.

The parent-location recorder has been removed. Older finds saved without a location offer a city-selection action on the find. Party also offers Add Missing Locations for selected older finds. Choose where those finds were actually spotted to add their points.

Quick help

GPS is not finding my location

Make sure Location Services is enabled for Farfetched Finds in iOS Settings and that the app is allowed to use location while open. Try again outdoors or near a window. You can always choose City / Region and log the find manually without GPS.

How is a find scored?

Scoring model 18 runs entirely on the device. It weighs distance from the plate’s home boundary, registered-vehicle supply, nearby regional connections, isolation, and border or water travel barriers. Hawaii and other origins that require vehicle shipping use a shared shipping model.

Supported plate designs, personalization, eligible rare colors, and unusual vehicle types can add whole-point bonuses based on rarity. Vehicle rarity uses the region where you spot the vehicle, with national estimates when local data is unavailable. Police, fire, and ambulance vehicles include their government-plate status without an extra bonus.

A zero-point find still counts toward your collection. An explicitly selected city’s state is treated as the spotting region, including near coastlines and state borders. Existing finds are recalculated for this pre-launch scoring update using their saved distance and coarse region. Simply reconnecting to the internet does not change a score.

Why did my find earn zero points?

A regular plate spotted in its home state or region can earn zero points when no bonus details apply. The find is still saved and counts toward your collection. A new plate, color, or vehicle type can add collection progress, and eligible finds can advance a challenge.

How do I log an oversized load?

Choose Oversized load as the vehicle type. It is a standalone discovery worth 10 points; you do not need to identify the vehicle carrying it. Any eligible plate or color details are scored separately.

Why does a custom plate stay in Quick Log?

Quick Log keeps the most recently earned achievement plate until another event earns a new one, including across trips and app restarts. Tap the plate to see what earned it. Editing or deleting the supporting find can remove that award and reveal an earlier earned plate; if none remain, the display returns to PL8HUNT.

How do I change the five plates on a scout’s profile?

Open the scout’s profile and tap any of the five display plates to choose a find credited to that scout. You can feature the same find in more than one position. A new display starts with the scout’s first find in all five positions; existing choices are kept. These display choices do not add points.

What appears in the Active Trip highlight?

The highlight follows the latest trip find. If that find completes a collection, changes the lead, or beats the trip’s previous best score, it can feature that achievement. Otherwise it shows Latest Find. The first scored find sets the starting record; tying an existing best does not set a new one.

What does Full Game unlock, and how do I restore it?

The free road test includes ten saved finds and one trip. Full Game is a one-time purchase for unlimited finds and trips and Family Party hosting. Collections, profiles, viewing and editing existing finds, backups, and joining an invited party stay free. There are no ads or subscription.

Open More → Unlock the Full Game to see the App Store price or choose Restore Purchase. Use the Apple Account that owns the purchase, or an eligible Apple Family Sharing account. Family Sharing for purchases is separate from the app’s Family Party. If the product is unavailable, reconnect and try again. TestFlight purchases use Apple’s test environment and do not carry over to the released App Store app.

How do I create a challenge?

Open Party → Road Rivalries → Create. Choose a preset or make a custom checklist of 5–10 colors and vehicle types, choose the participating scouts, then review and start it. Only finds logged after the challenge starts count. One find can satisfy several checklist targets, and it still earns its normal points and collection progress.

My Family Party is not syncing

Confirm that each participant is signed in to iCloud, has iCloud Drive available, and is using the Family Party invitation sent by the owner. Keep the app open briefly after reconnecting. Offline changes are saved and retried when service returns.

How do I enable or change notifications?

Open the More tab and find Notifications. Allow notifications when asked, then choose whether another scout’s rare finds of 20 points or more and newly added scouts should alert you. Comments by another scout are always eligible for an alert. If permission was denied, use Open iOS Settings to change it. Your own activity does not create an alert on the same party.

A scout or find is missing on another device

First verify that both devices joined the same shared Family Party—not two separately created parties. Then check the sync status in the Party tab and allow both devices time to reconnect. The current scout selection, preferred location method, and saved logging location intentionally stay local to each device. A private iCloud backup is a recovery copy, not a live Family Party connection.

How do I protect or move my data?

Automatic private iCloud backups are enabled when iCloud is available. In More → Data & recovery → iCloud Backup, check the last successful backup, back up now, turn automatic backups on or off, or choose an earlier recovery copy. A fresh installation using the same iCloud account checks for saved progress automatically. If this device already has progress, choose whether to restore a cloud copy or keep the local game. Cloud restores disconnect Family Party on this device; you can rejoin from Party. Wait for Family Party sync to finish before restoring.

If iCloud is full, local play continues, but new progress is not protected by a cloud backup until an upload succeeds. Free up space, upgrade your iCloud storage through Apple, or export a backup to a computer or another storage provider. Copies in iCloud Drive also use iCloud storage. An erase can lose changes made since the last successful backup.

In the More tab, choose Back Up Data and save the resulting .fffbackup file somewhere you control. Restore Backup validates a selected file before replacing data on the device. Create a fresh backup before restoring another one.

Why does a find show only a city or region?

This is intentional. Automatic GPS is used temporarily for scoring, but the exact coordinates are discarded. The saved find keeps only a coarse place label and the information needed to explain its score.

Can I correct or remove a find?

Open the previous find to edit it. You can also delete a find; deletion removes it from its trip, credited scout profiles, and collections. Totals, challenge progress, highlights, and earned achievement plates are updated from the remaining finds. Consider creating a backup before making a large change.

Contact support

Email farfetchedfindssupport@gmail.com. Include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The Farfetched Finds version shown in Settings or the App Store.
  • The steps you took, what you expected, and what happened.
  • A screenshot if it explains the issue and contains no private information.
Protect your family’s privacy. Do not attach backup files or include scout names, profile photos, exact locations, iCloud invitation links, or other personal information unless it is necessary to resolve the issue.

Plate artwork and rights concerns

Plate designs, artwork, seals, logos, and trademarks belong to their respective rights holders. Farfetched Finds is an independent spotting game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any issuing authority or rights holder.

Plate images help identify and record finds. They include older designs and edited artwork and may differ from plates currently issued. Open More → Plate Artwork & Rights in the app for source-photo credits, license links, modification notices, and each artwork reference. A source-photo license does not necessarily grant rights in the designs or marks depicted. Farfetched Finds offers its contributions to CC BY and CC BY-SA image adaptations under the same license shown for the source; those image licenses do not cover the app’s code or unrelated content.

If you hold or represent rights in an image or depicted design, email farfetchedfindssupport@gmail.com with:

  • The artwork reference from the app, or the issuing region and design. Include a screenshot or source link if needed to identify it.
  • The work or mark concerned, the rights you hold, and your authority to act for the rights holder if applicable.
  • The correction or other action you request and contact information for a reply.
  • The app version if the concern relates to an image in the app.

We review notices promptly and may request clarification. When a concern is substantiated, we will take appropriate action, which may include correcting attribution, replacing artwork with a generic representation, or removing the artwork. We will explain the action and its expected timing in our reply.

Artwork changes are delivered in app updates. Previously installed versions and saved images may retain earlier artwork. Replacing an illustration does not require deleting players’ finds or collection progress.

Please omit private gameplay or family information. The report links open your email app; Farfetched Finds does not automatically attach finds, backups, photos, or account data.

Road safety

Farfetched Finds is a passenger activity. Do not look for plates, type, or interact with the app while driving. Pull over legally and park before using the app if no passenger can log a find.
