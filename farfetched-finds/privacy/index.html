Privacy Policy

Your road-trip memories belong to you.

Farfetched Finds is designed to work without advertising, behavioral tracking, or a developer-operated account system.

Effective September 2, 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version

Farfetched Finds does not save exact GPS coordinates, license-plate numbers, or vehicle photos. Gameplay data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up, share it through another app, or enable a private iCloud Family Party.

The app has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal information.

Information the app uses

Gameplay and profile information

The app saves the information you enter or select, including scout names, optional avatar emoji or colors, optional profile photos, trip names, plate jurisdictions and designs, vehicle details, find times, coarse place labels, scores, profile showcases, and archived trips.

Location

If you choose Automatic GPS, the app requests a one-time location while you log a find. It uses that location on the device to calculate distance and produce a coarse city or region label. The exact latitude and longitude are then discarded and are not written to the saved find or its backup. The saved find may include the coarse place or region, the location accuracy measurement, and the distance used for scoring.

If you choose City / Region, the app uses the approximate center of the place you select from its bundled offline place catalog and does not request the device’s GPS location for that find.

Photos

If you choose a scout profile photo, Apple’s photo picker gives the app access only to the image you select. The app downsizes that image and stores it as part of the scout profile. Taking or saving photos of vehicles or license plates is not part of the app.

iCloud status

The app checks whether iCloud is available when you use Family Party synchronization. Apple handles your iCloud identity; Farfetched Finds does not create a separate username, password, or developer-operated customer account.

How information is used

  • Calculate and explain scores.
  • Display finds, collections, leaderboards, profiles, and trip history.
  • Save and restore app data on your device.
  • Synchronize a Family Party when its owner explicitly enables iCloud sharing.
  • Create a backup or sharing item only when you ask the app to do so.

The app does not use this information for advertising, cross-app tracking, credit decisions, or data brokerage.

Storage and sharing

On your device

Gameplay data is stored in the app’s private Application Support area. Saved files use iOS Complete File Protection. The app keeps a local recovery copy so it can restore the most recent valid save if the primary file becomes damaged.

iCloud Family Party

Family Party sharing is optional. When the owner enables it, the party’s scouts, profile information, finds, trips, settings, and featured profile finds are stored as an encrypted CloudKit asset in the owner’s private iCloud database and shared only with people the owner invites through Apple’s CloudKit sharing interface. Invited participants can view and update the shared party according to the permissions the owner grants.

The device’s current-scout selection, onboarding status, and preferred location method remain local rather than synchronizing with the party.

Backups and the system share sheet

You can export a backup containing scouts, finds, profiles, and trips. The backup does not include exact coordinates. You decide where that file is saved and who can access it. When you share a find or rendered profile, Apple’s system share sheet sends it only to the destination you choose. A find’s coarse location is excluded by default and added only if you turn on the location option before sharing.

Apple services

CloudKit, iCloud sharing, background CloudKit notifications, the photo picker, location permission, and the share sheet are Apple services governed by Apple’s privacy practices. No non-Apple analytics, advertising, or social-network SDK is included in the app.

Retention, deletion, and choices

  • You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings and continue using manual City / Region entry.
  • You can replace or remove a scout profile photo in the app by choosing an emoji, color, or initial instead.
  • You can edit or delete individual finds. Ending a trip can archive its history for later use.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data, subject to device backups managed by you or Apple. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them there.
  • CloudKit party data remains in the owner’s iCloud account until the owner removes the shared data using Apple’s iCloud controls. Removing a participant ends that participant’s access but does not itself erase the owner’s copy.

Because Family Party records live in a user’s private or shared iCloud database rather than a customer database operated by the developer, the developer cannot directly retrieve or delete those private records for you. If you need help finding the applicable controls, visit Farfetched Finds Support.

Family use

Farfetched Finds is intended for families, but it does not require full legal names, profile photos, or exact locations. Parents and guardians should decide what information to enter for younger scouts and who may join a Family Party. Initials, emoji, and color avatars are available when a photograph is not appropriate.

Passenger safety: Never interact with the app while driving. A passenger should log finds, or the vehicle should be safely parked.

This website

This support website does not use advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as an IP address, to provide and secure the hosting service under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Policy changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described clearly.

Questions about this policy can be emailed to farfetchedfindssupport@gmail.com. Please do not include scout names, profile photos, exported backups, or precise location information unless it is necessary to answer your question.
